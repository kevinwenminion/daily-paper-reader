## 摘要
人工智能通过高效探索化学与结构空间加速了材料预测与设计，尤其在新材料发现方面前景广阔。然而，材料发现中的新颖性涵盖化学合理性、结构独特性、性能相关性及实验可实现性，这使得基于人工智能的新颖性主张难以得到证实。我们引入了一个材料属性层次体系，从固有的、由成分决定的属性到外禀的、依赖于加工的性能，以阐明部署约束并区分结构新颖性、物理新颖性及部署新颖性。该框架为涵盖化学成分、微观结构、加工工艺以及测试与表征的多模态材料数据提供了基于证据的视角，表明当前证据仍主要集中在成分与理想化结构上，而异质性、代表性不足且弱整合的多模态限制了其对物理新颖性与部署新颖性的支撑。同时，它也凸显了主要依赖计算标签与代理新颖性标准的基准测试的局限性。需要建立社区范围的数据收集、模态对齐与证据综合标准，以支持多模态数据构建、过程感知的多模态建模、可行性优先的生成建模以及部署感知的基准测试，从而使生成式与多模态人工智能能够设计出具有可信的科学与实践新颖性的实验可实现材料。

## Abstract
Artificial intelligence (AI) is accelerating materials prediction and design by enabling efficient exploration of chemical and structural spaces, with particular promise for novel materials discovery. However, novelty in materials discovery encompasses chemical plausibility, structural distinctiveness, property relevance and experimental realisability, making AI-driven novelty claims difficult to substantiate. We introduce a materials property hierarchy, from intrinsic, composition-determined properties to extrinsic, processing-dependent performance, to clarify deployment constraints and distinguish structural, physical and deployment novelty. This framework motivates an evidence-based view of multimodal materials data spanning chemical composition, microstructure, processing, and testing and characterisation, showing that current evidence remains concentrated in composition and idealised structure while heterogeneous, under-represented and weakly integrated modalities limit support for physical and deployment novelty. It also highlights the limitations of benchmarks based mainly on computational labels and proxy novelty criteria. Community-wide standards for data collection, modality alignment and evidence synthesis are needed to support multimodal data construction, process-aware multimodal modelling, feasibility-first generative modelling and deployment-aware benchmarking, so that generative and multimodal AI can design experimentally realisable materials with defensible scientific and practical novelty.
