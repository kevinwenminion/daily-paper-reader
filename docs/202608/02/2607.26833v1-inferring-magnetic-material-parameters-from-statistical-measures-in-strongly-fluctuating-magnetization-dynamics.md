## 摘要
磁性材料参数如交换刚度和磁各向异性决定磁性系统的行为与功能，但从磁化数据中局部推断这些参数仍具挑战性，尤其是在具有多晶或多相微结构的强波动区域，传统基于织构的方法在此不可靠。我们提出了一种仅基于磁化的框架，从热驱动的磁化动力学中推断材料参数。利用微磁学模拟，我们从磁化动力学中提取时间均值和潜在熵等统计量，将这些描述符拟合模型，并反转模型以推断材料参数。我们展示了该框架能够在异质样品中实现材料参数推断以及晶界检测。在所考虑的描述符中，潜在熵比时间均值产生更准确的参数估计。我们的结果确立了潜在熵作为一种高效的描述符，用于从动态磁化数据中推断磁性材料参数，并指向其在高温及更广泛强波动条件下实验参数提取中的应用。

## Abstract
Magnetic material parameters such as the exchange stiffness and magnetic anisotropy govern the behavior and functionality of magnetic systems, yet their local inference from magnetization data remains challenging, particularly in strongly fluctuating regimes with polycrystalline or multiphase microstructure, where conventional texture-based methods become unreliable. We introduce a magnetization-only framework for inferring material parameters from thermally driven magnetization dynamics. Using micromagnetic simulations, we extract statistical quantities such as temporal mean and latent entropy from the magnetization dynamics, fit models to these descriptors, and invert the models to infer material parameters. We show that this framework enables material-parameter inference as well as grain-boundary detection in a heterogeneous sample. Among the descriptors considered, latent entropy yields more accurate parameter estimates than the temporal mean. Our results establish latent entropy as an efficient descriptor for inferring magnetic material parameters from dynamical magnetization data and point toward its use for experimental parameter extraction at high temperatures and, more broadly, under strongly fluctuating conditions.
