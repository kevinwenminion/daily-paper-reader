## 摘要
复杂和高熵合金目前备受关注，因为它们在恶劣环境下的力学和耐腐蚀性能，特别是无碳能源应用所需的性能。然而，由于其复杂性和鸡尾酒效应，采用试错法以块体和薄膜形式制备它们是不切实际的。人工智能的最新发展为其制备和性能调整带来了新的可能性。首先，我们概述了材料与数据科学研究。然后，我们描述了DIADEM——法国材料与数据科学融合计划——如何通过建立全国性的合成与表征平台网络（DIADEM发现中心）来推动无碳能源应用（核能、高温电解等）创新涂层的开发。我们特别描述了DIADEM-2D，这是一种AI驱动的混合HiPIMS/脉冲直流PVD工艺，采用4个阴极的共焦组合配置。我们利用文献数据，展示了在熔盐介质和核事故条件下耐腐蚀高熵合金的确定。我们实现了一个与元素无关的模型，汇集了沉积参数和涂层性能，从而能够设计特定成分的保护涂层。我们证明了该工艺的可行性及其准确性。

## Abstract
Complex and high entropy alloys are attracting much attention currently thanks to their mechanical and corrosion resistance properties in harsh environments, in particular needed for carbon-free energy applications. However, their elaboration in bulk and in thin film form in a trial-and-error approach is impractical due to their complexity and the cocktail effect. The recent development of artificial intelligence brings a new possibility for their elaboration and adjustment of their properties.   Firstly, we present an overview of Materials and data science research. Then we describe how DIADEM - French initiative for Materials and Data science convergence - tackles the development of innovative coatings for carbon-free energy applications (nuclear, high temperature electrolysis, ...) thanks to the development of a nationwide network of synthesis and characterization platforms - the DIADEM discovery hub. We describe in particular DIADEM-2D, an AI-driven Hybrid HiPIMS/Pulsed-DC PVD process using 4 cathodes in confocal combinatorial configuration. We present the high entropy alloy determination using data from the literature for corrosion resistance in molten salt media and nuclear accidental conditions. An element-independent model gathering deposition parameters and coating properties has been implemented allowing the design of protective coatings with a particular composition. We demonstrate the feasibility of this process and its accuracy.
