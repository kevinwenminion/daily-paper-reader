## 摘要
我们提出了一种基于扩散的生成框架，用于可控生成具有低平均曲率残差的三周期极小曲面（TPMS）结构。现有的TPMS生成方法通常局限于少量规范族，或产生偏离精确极小性的类TPMS近似。为了实现该生成框架，我们首先通过枚举可镜像基本包围体上的允许边界环并求解多样极小曲面片，构建了一个包含超过18,000个独特TPMS的大规模数据集。每个曲面随后被投影到一个紧凑的傅里叶潜空间中，该空间显式地强制周期性和$D_{2h}$对称性。接着，在该潜空间中训练一个基于Transformer的扩散模型，以支持无条件采样、确定性反演、局部编辑以及在用户指定约束下的条件生成。实验表明，该模型生成多样、低曲率的TPMS候选，在条件约束下满足稀疏几何约束并匹配目标均质化线弹性属性，为TPMS逆设计提供了实用工具。

## Abstract
We propose a diffusion-based generative framework for controllable generation of triply periodic minimal surface (TPMS) structures with low residual mean curvature.Existing TPMS generation approaches are often restricted to a small set of canonical families or produce TPMS-like approximations that deviate from exact minimality. To enable this generative framework, we first construct a large-scale dataset of over 18K unique TPMS by enumerating admissible boundary loops on mirrorable fundamental bounding volumes and solving for diverse minimal-surface patches. Each surface is then projected onto a compact Fourier latent space that explicitly enforces periodicity and $D_{2h}$ symmetry. Next, a transformer-based diffusion model is trained in this latent space to support unconditional sampling, deterministic inversion, local editing, and conditional generation under user-specified constraints. Experiments demonstrate that the model generates diverse, low-curvature TPMS candidates that, under conditioning, satisfy sparse geometric constraints and match target homogenized linear elastic properties, providing a practical tool for TPMS inverse design.
